FIRST-TIME OAUTH SETUP

Give sign-in a way back.

Client credentials identify your app. You also need to register its exact callback address with each provider.

01

Register your YouTube callback

Open Google Cloud → Credentials ↗. Edit the Web application OAuth client matching your .env file. Add this under Authorized redirect URIs and save.

Enable YouTube Data API v3. If the consent screen is in testing, add the Google account you want to connect as a test user.

02

Set up Instagram with HTTPS

Use a public HTTPS domain or a development tunnel pointing to this service. Set BASE_URL in .env to that address, restart Relay, and open the workspace through that same address.

Open Meta for Developers → your app ↗, then Instagram → API setup with Instagram login → Business login settings. Add the HTTPS callback under OAuth redirect URIs and save.

Use a Business or Creator account. The Instagram App ID and secret must come from the Instagram Login setup.

03

Choose an account in the sign-in window

Allow popups for your workspace, then click Connect. Google will ask which account to use. After consent, the sign-in window closes and the account list updates.

URLs must match exactly: HTTPS vs HTTP, hostname, port, path, and trailing slash. After changing BASE_URL, register the new YouTube callback too. Your browser may show the sign-in popup as a separate tab.
